New members join on the nomination of a Full Member. Membership is free; members contribute in kind through staff time, data, computing and hosting activities.

Member Benefits

What members gain from joining

Conference roles

Special sessions, programme committee seats and a PhD forum for member students at EIDT and SIMC.

Publication roles

Priority invitations to special issues, and roles as guest editors, session chairs and programme committee members.

Shared resources

Access to TIIS datasets, code, models, computing and teaching materials under agreed terms of use.

Projects and funding

Members-only seed project calls, partners for joint grant proposals and letters of support.

Mobility and training

Priority for exchange visits, internships and co-supervision; reduced or waived fees at the Summer/Winter School.

Voice and visibility

A vote in the General Assembly, the right to stand for Vice-Chair, Working Group leadership and a lab profile.
