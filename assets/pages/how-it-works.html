How It Works

One collaboration cycle, repeated across the network

1

Shared problem

A member or partner brings a real research question.

2

Joint project

Two or more labs form a seed or flagship project.

3

People move

Lecturers and students visit, intern and co-supervise across labs.

4

Resources shared

Data, code, models and computing flow between labs.

5

Results shared

The conference, publications and open assets reach the whole network.

Step 5 feeds step 1: new partners and new problems start the next cycle.
