Our Vision

Our vision

“Intelligent infrastructures and systems that people can understand, verify and trust.”

Mission

To connect Computer departments across Asia-Pacific and beyond through joint research, the exchange of people and the sharing of resources for transparent, trustworthy and human-centred intelligent systems.

Objectives

Five things members do together

01

Joint research

Member labs define and carry out research projects together, from shared problems to co-designed prototypes.

02

Exchange of people

Lecturers, researchers and students move between labs for visits, internships and co-supervision.

03

Resource sharing

Datasets, code, models, computing, lab equipment and teaching materials, shared under agreed terms.

04

Technology leadership

Lead the development and adoption of transparent AI technologies, open benchmarks and good practice in the region.

05

A common forum

The EIDT and SIMC conferences and a Summer/Winter School bring the network together.
