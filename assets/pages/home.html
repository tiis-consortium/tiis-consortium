Welcome to TIIS

A collaborative network for the next generation of intelligent systems.

The Transparent Intelligent Infrastructures and Systems (TIIS) Consortium brings together computer science departments, researchers and partner institutions to foster collaboration in transparent and trustworthy intelligent infrastructures.

Through joint research, education, academic exchange and international events, TIIS promotes responsible innovation and the development of intelligent systems that serve people and society.
