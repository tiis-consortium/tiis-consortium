TIIS Consortium

Transparent Intelligent Infrastructures and Systems

General Chair: Vinh Truong Hoang · Ho Chi Minh City Open University, Vietnam

WHY TIIS

Intelligent infrastructure must be understandable to be trusted

TIIS connects researchers and institutions to advance transparent, trustworthy, and human-centered intelligent systems through global collaboration.

01

AI runs critical systems

Transport, energy, health, agriculture and public services increasingly depend on decisions made by AI.

02

Opacity erodes trust

When a system cannot be explained, failures go unnoticed and its decisions cannot be checked or contested.

03

No lab can do it alone

Data, computing, domain partners and evaluation capacity are spread across many institutions.
